03 Live it 1,000 times

One trade is luck. A thousand is math.

We simulate this exact sauda across thousands of random expiries drawn from its own volatility, then subtract every charge. This is the distribution the SEBI number is made of.

LOSS outcomes PROFIT outcomes average result

This is an education tool, not advice. Sauda does not send orders, hold money, or recommend trades. Figures are modelled estimates: option value at expiry is approximated by intrinsic value, the index is modelled as drift-free lognormal from the IV you enter, and charges follow standard Indian discount-broker rates (flat ₹20/order brokerage, STT 0.10% on the sell leg, exchange & SEBI turnover fees, 18% GST, 0.003% stamp). Real fills, taxes, lot sizes and slippage differ. Verify on your broker before trading. Derivatives can lose you more than you put in.

Model & assumptions
  • Underlying at expiry: S·exp(−½σ²T + σ√T·Z), Z~N(0,1), T = days/365, no drift.
  • Probability of profit uses the lognormal CDF with breakeven adjusted for round-trip cost per unit.
  • Monte Carlo: 6,000 draws; exit modelled as squaring off at intrinsic value; worthless longs lapse (no exit charge).
  • Lot sizes (approx.): Nifty 75, Bank Nifty 35, Fin Nifty 65, Sensex 20 — confirm current values with the exchange.
